# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Adults nationwide who are tired of generic workout and nutrition plans and want direct, human accountability. Layne also serves people within a 25-mile radius of Cleveland, Texas through the same phone and Zoom coaching model.

## Product Purpose

Layne Fitness turns interest into a scheduled conversation with Layne. The site explains personalized fitness and nutrition coaching, proves that Layne has lived through his own transformation, answers common objections, and makes booking or calling effortless.

## Brand Personality

Direct, kinetic, accountable. The site should feel like a disciplined performance lab run by a real young coach, not a supplement storefront, generic gym template, or influencer funnel.

## Anti-references

- Generic black-and-red bodybuilding templates.
- Repetitive glass cards, neon gradients, emoji icons, and vague hype.
- Medical-clinic styling or unsupported health claims.
- Fake testimonials, credentials, scarcity, ratings, or guaranteed results.

## Design Principles

1. Show the transformation before explaining the service.
2. Make the next action unmistakable on every screen.
3. Sell human judgment and adjustment, not another static template.
4. Use proof and plain answers instead of adjectives.
5. Treat safety boundaries as a trust signal.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA with keyboard-complete interactions, visible focus, reduced-motion alternatives, logical headings, adequate contrast, 200% zoom reflow, descriptive media text, and no content that relies on color alone.
